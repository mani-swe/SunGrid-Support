## 4. Installation, Engineering & Net Metering Workflow

Installing a SunGrid power system follows a structured 6-phase engineering pipeline. Total project duration typically ranges between 4 to 8 weeks, heavily dependent on municipal permit processing speeds.

```
[Phase 1: Site Audit] ➔ [Phase 2: CAD Design] ➔ [Phase 3: Permitting] 
      ➔ [Phase 4: Physical Install] ➔ [Phase 5: Inspection] ➔ [Phase 6: PTO]
```

### 4.1 Detailed Project Phases
1. **Site Assessment & Engineering Audit (Days 1–3):**
   A SunGrid field technician performs LiDAR rooftop measurements, structural rafter analysis, shading calculations using Solmetric SunEye, and main panel rating inspection.
2. **CAD Engineering & Electrical Design (Days 4–8):**
   Our certified electrical engineers draft the Single Line Diagram (SLD) and structural site attachment plan.
3. **Permitting & Utility Interconnection Application (Weeks 2–4):**
   Documentation is submitted to the local Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) for building/electrical permits and to the utility company for Net Energy Metering (NEM) pre-approval.
4. **Physical Installation (1–2 Days):**
   SunGrid certified installers mount roof flashing, racking structures, secure solar modules, mount the SunGrid Hybrid-X inverter, and wire storage/conduit runs.
5. **AHJ Inspection & Safety Verification (Week 5–6):**
   The local municipal inspector verifies electrical safety, rapid shutdown compliance, and roof attachments.
6. **Permission to Operate (PTO) & Commissioning (Week 6–8):**
   The utility installs a bi-directional smart meter and issues formal Permission to Operate (PTO). The system is activated, and telemetry syncs with the SunGrid Mobile App.

### 4.2 Net Metering (NEM) Principles
SunGrid systems are engineered for full integration with regional Net Energy Metering (NEM 2.0 / NEM 3.0) frameworks:
- **Excess Generation (Daytime):** Surplus solar electricity is pushed to the public grid. The utility credits the customer’s billing account at retail or avoided-cost rates.
- **Consumption (Nighttime):** When solar production drops to zero, electricity is imported seamlessly from the utility grid, consuming accumulated net credits.
- **Time-of-Use (TOU) Optimization:** For customers on NEM 3.0 or TOU rates, SunGrid PowerVault automatically discharges battery energy during peak tariff hours (e.g., 4:00 PM – 9:00 PM) to avoid buying expensive utility grid power.

## 7. Customer Support Knowledge Base & Troubleshooting FAQs

### Q1: What happens during a main utility power outage?
**Answer:** Standard grid-tied solar systems automatically switch off during power grid failures due to anti-islanding safety mandates (protecting utility utility line workers). However, if your system includes a **SunGrid PowerVault Battery**, the system automatically isolates from the grid within 10 milliseconds, providing continuous backup power to designated critical circuits (refrigerators, Wi-Fi, lights, outlets).

### Q2: Why is my solar power production lower on cloudy or rainy days?
**Answer:** Solar PV panels rely on irradiance (photons), not heat or direct sunlight. During overcast or rainy weather, panels continue producing electricity from diffuse light, generating approximately **15% to 30%** of their rated capacity depending on cloud density.

### Q3: How do I read the metrics on my SunGrid Mobile App?
**Answer:** The primary app dashboard displays three live energy streams:
- **Solar Yield (Green):** Instantaneous DC-to-AC power being generated by your rooftop panels.
- **Home Demand (Blue):** Real-time electrical power consumption of your property.
- **Grid Balance (Orange):** Shows whether you are exporting excess energy (positive number/outward arrow) or importing grid energy (negative number/inward arrow).

### Q4: How do I resolve "Inverter Error Code E-104: Grid Voltage Out of Bounds"?
**Answer:** Error E-104 occurs when local utility grid line voltage spikes beyond safe operational thresholds (typically above 253V AC).
1. **Step 1:** Wait 15 minutes. Grid transient spikes often settle automatically.
2. **Step 2:** If the error persists, perform a system hard reset:
   - Switch OFF the SunGrid Hybrid-X AC isolator switch.
   - Switch OFF the DC disconnect switch located under the inverter.
   - Wait 120 seconds.
   - Switch ON the DC disconnect first, then switch ON the AC isolator.
3. **Step 3:** If the error code reoccurs, contact SunGrid Support to request a remote grid parameter adjustment or utility voltage log report.

### Q5: How do I claim the 30% Federal Investment Tax Credit (ITC)?
**Answer:** File **IRS Form 5695** ("Residential Energy Credits") alongside your annual tax return. SunGrid supplies a itemized final proof-of-purchase invoice upon system activation showing total eligible equipment and labor expenditures. *Disclaimer: Always consult a certified CPA or tax professional regarding personal tax credit eligibility.*
